Cartefield is a chunky 70s funk script with wide, connected, super-bold letterforms.

Thick rounded strokes, generous bowls, and a slight forward lean — the kind of script you find on a chocolate-bar wrapper or a soul-record sleeve from 1973. Lowercase joins are smooth and the descenders are pronounced, giving it a deep groovy presence on a single line.

Imagine it across the front of a foil-stamped vinyl sleeve with a sunburst printed behind it.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.

Designed and created by Måns Grebäck.
